Irish Zinc

Overview

The Irish Zinc Project is focused on prospective stratigraphy analogous to major zinc-lead deposits within the Irish Midlands Orefield.

Geological Setting

Mineralisation is associated with carbonate host rocks proximal to basin-bounding structures. Historical datasets indicate anomalous Zn+Pb soil geochemistry.

Program

Current work comprises validation sampling, re-logging historic core (where accessible) and integrating multi-element geochemistry with structure.

Next Steps

Refine structural corridors, complete limited geophysics to delineate alteration, and prioritise shallow test drilling.
