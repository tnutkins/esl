Irish Lithium

Overview

Early-stage evaluation of pegmatite swarms with potential for lithium-bearing mineral assemblages.

Geological Setting

Pegmatites intrude metamorphic country rock; fractionation indicators and accessory mineralogy guide vectoring.

Program

Orientation geochemistry, petrographic analysis and remote spectral screening are underway to isolate priority dykes.

Next Steps

Channel sampling of mapped pegmatites followed by initial subsurface testing of highest fractionation index targets.
