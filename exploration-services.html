Exploration Services

We offer integrated consulting and execution capabilities across the mineral exploration lifecycle.

Capabilities

  • Target generation & concept evaluation
  • Geochemical survey design & QA/QC
  • Structural & lithological mapping
  • Sampling (soil, stream sediment, rock chip, trench)
  • Data management & validation
  • GIS & 3D modelling integration
  • Exploration program budgeting & scheduling
  • Stakeholder & land access coordination
  • Technical reporting & compliance documentation

Workflow

  1. Desk Study: Compilation of historical data and public domain datasets.
  2. Screening: Geochemical orientation, reconnaissance mapping.
  3. Refinement: Integrated interpretation, target ranking.
  4. Testing: Ground truthing, trenching, early drilling (if justified).
  5. Review: Decision gates with technical and ESG criteria.

Contact us to discuss bespoke scopes aligned with your project's stage.
